How HeritageGuard Works

A simple reporting process designed to help people document and respond to heritage threats.

1
📝

Report

Submit a report about suspected illegal excavation, artifact removal, site damage or another heritage threat.

2
📍

Provide the location and useful details so the issue can be identified and understood.

3

🛡

Verified cases can support appropriate action and collaboration with responsible authorities.
